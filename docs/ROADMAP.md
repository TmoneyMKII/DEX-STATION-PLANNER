# Roadmap

The end goal: design a station, simulate how it is supplied and operated, then press **Generate** to get the complete document package for funders, the NSF, partners and operators.

## ✅ Milestone 1: Foundation (v0.1)
Module designer (grid, straight and curved walls, openings, connectors, equipment, elevation, 3D preview), station builder (snapping connectors, corridors, utility runs), globe placement, project files, undo/redo, themes and units.

## Milestone 2: Site infrastructure
- Place **airfields/skiways, helipads, ports/piers, roads, fuel farms, cargo yards and science sites** on the site plan and globe.
- Aircraft, vessel and vehicle catalogs (e.g. LC-130, Twin Otter, Basler BT-67, helicopters, icebreakers, tractor traverses) with payload, range and runway requirements.
- Seasonal access windows by environment (polar summer, monsoon, high-altitude weather).
- Terrain-aware placement (slope and elevation from ion terrain) and real module 3D models (glTF) on the globe.

## Milestone 3: Logistics & operations simulation
- Discrete-event simulation of flights, sea lifts and traverses moving people, fuel, food and **science experiments** in and out.
- Population and rotation schedules, consumables burn (fuel, food, water), cargo manifests.
- Bottleneck and risk reporting; animated playback on the globe.

## Milestone 4: Document generation
- One-click PDF package: executive summary, design schematics (floor plans, site plan, elevations), equipment schedules, mass and power budgets, logistics plan, operations concept, risk register.
- Templates for different audiences (NSF-style proposal sections, financing, partner briefings).

## Milestone 5: Engineering budgets
- Heat loss and HVAC sizing by climate, power profiles over the day and season, water and waste balances, snow drift guidance for elevated buildings.

## Milestone 6: Beyond Earth
- Moon and Mars globes, gravity and logistics models for extraterrestrial sites.

## Ongoing
- Offline tile caching for field use, installer (MSIX/winget) and file association, localization, accessibility.
